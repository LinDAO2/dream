How this works
  • Dream Pages asks questions. It doesn't tell you what a dream means; only you know that.
  • Notes about images are common associations, offered as reflections. Dreams aren't predictions.
  • It is for reflection. It does not diagnose, predict or treat, and it isn't a crisis service.
  • No scores and no counting against you. Write when it helps; rest when you need to.
  • Change comes in small steps: a feeling named, a question sat with, a tiny experiment tried.
  • Your writing is saved on this device only. No account, no tracking. If you turn on AI questions or handwriting reading, that dream or photo is sent to the AI service you set up, and only then.
  • Voice typing is handled by your browser or phone, which may use its own online speech service.
Rhythm

A rest day is a day with nothing asked of you. Write if you want to; rest if you don't.

A cue for the morning

Tie writing to something you already do. A steady cue helps a habit take root more than willpower does.

A gentle reminder · optional

One quiet notification at a time you choose, only on days you haven't written yet. Never more than one a day, and nothing else. It stays off unless you turn it on.

AI questions · optional

The questions are written by a small interpreter that lives inside this page and works offline. If you like, add your own OpenAI-compatible key for questions shaped more closely to your dream.

Your key is kept only in this browser's storage. With AI on, the dream you explore, or a handwriting photo you add, is sent straight to the address below with your key. Nothing else is sent: your other pages, reviews, letters and check-ins stay here.

Your archive

Your mornings live only in this browser. Keep a backup now and then, or use one to move to another device.

Support

If things feel like too much, you don't have to carry it alone. Talking with someone can help.

  • In the US, call or text 988, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, any time.
  • Elsewhere, findahelpline.com lists free, confidential helplines by country.
  • If someone is hurting you: in the US, the National Domestic Violence Hotline is 1-800-799-7233, or text START to 88788.
  • If you're in danger right now, call your local emergency number.

Dream Pages can't answer what you write the way a person can, and no one is watching over it.
